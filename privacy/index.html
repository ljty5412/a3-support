隐私政策

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 29 日

适用范围

本政策说明 A3: Veildeal 游戏、支持联系和本支持网站涉及的数据处理。开发者展示名：浪迹天涯。联系邮箱：3173374170@qq.com。

游戏与开发者服务

开发者不运营 A3 账号系统、统计分析服务、广告服务、支付服务或专用游戏服务器。游戏不会把本地设置或对局记录自动上传到开发者控制的服务器。你主动通过邮件提供的信息另见下文“联系支持”。

本地设置、战绩与教学进度

游戏在你的电脑上保存昵称、语言、牌桌主题、显示与音频偏好、自定义音乐路径，以及教学进度和完成标记。设置使用操作系统的本地设置存储；战绩使用本地文件保存。

本地战绩包括游戏局数、第一名次数、已记录对局的阵营胜场与名次累计、当前及最佳连胜、单人阵营对局与胜场、单张手牌翻盘次数、各牌型出牌次数及统计覆盖局数。这些累计数据用于战绩展示和成就判定，不是开发者运营的远程分析服务。

回放与诊断日志

房主组件可能在房主电脑写入对局回放和诊断日志，用于查看对局和排查故障。文件可能包含对局过程、玩家昵称及连接或错误信息。分享前请检查内容，只提供与问题有关的部分并遮去无关个人信息。

Steam Cloud

当 Steam Cloud 可用且已启用时，游戏通过 Valve 的 Steam Remote Storage 同步两个文件：战绩文件 a3_profile_stats_v2.json 和可移植偏好文件 a3_settings_v1.json。后者包含昵称、语言、牌桌主题、全屏偏好、音乐与音效开关及音量。

自定义音乐文件与路径、教学进度、回放和诊断日志不包含在这两个同步文件中。你可以在 Steam 中关闭 Cloud；游戏仍在本机保存数据。实际同步取决于 Steam 服务与相关设置。

局域网与 Steam 联机

创建或加入局域网牌局时，游戏会与同一本地网络中的其他玩家通信。局域网流量未加密。房间口令只用于防止误入，请勿复用其他账号密码。

使用 Steam 功能时，Steam 身份、好友、大厅、网络连接和成就等相关数据由 Valve 的服务处理。对局流量可能直接连接，也可能经 Valve 网络中继。Valve 按其 Steam 隐私政策处理相关数据；相关功能取决于服务可用性和设置。A3 不会获得你的 Steam 密码或 Steam Guard 凭据。

网站访问

本网站页面没有联系表单、分析脚本、广告追踪、第三方字体或脚本，也不设置 Cookie。邮箱链接仅在你点击时交由你的邮件应用处理，不会自动发送邮件。本支持网站由 GitHub Pages 托管。GitHub 会为安全目的记录并存储访问者 IP 地址，无论访问者是否登录 GitHub。相关处理请参阅 GitHub 隐私声明及 GitHub Pages 数据收集说明。

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Scope

This policy describes data handling related to the A3: Veildeal game, support contact and this support website. Developer display name: 浪迹天涯. Contact email: 3173374170@qq.com.

The game and developer services

The developer does not operate an A3 account system, analytics service, advertising service, payment service or dedicated game server. The game does not automatically upload local settings or gameplay records to a developer-controlled server. Information you voluntarily send by email is described under “Contacting support” below.

Local settings, statistics and tutorial progress

The game saves your nickname, language, table theme, display and audio preferences, custom-music path, and tutorial progress and completion flags on your computer. Settings use the operating system's local settings storage; statistics are saved in a local file.

Local statistics include games played, first-place finishes, team wins and accumulated finishing ranks for tracked matches, current and best win streaks, solo-role games and wins, one-card comebacks, counts of each combination played, and the number of matches covered by these statistics. These accumulated records support the statistics display and achievement checks; they are not a remote analytics service operated by the developer.

Replays and diagnostic logs

The host component may write match replays and diagnostic logs on the host computer to review matches and troubleshoot problems. Files may contain match events, player nicknames and connection or error information. Check files before sharing, include only relevant parts and hide unrelated personal information.

Steam Cloud

When Steam Cloud is available and enabled, the game uses Valve's Steam Remote Storage to synchronize two files: a3_profile_stats_v2.json for statistics and a3_settings_v1.json for portable preferences. The latter contains your nickname, language, table theme, fullscreen preference, and music and sound switches and volumes.

Custom music files and paths, tutorial progress, replays and diagnostic logs are not included in these two synchronized files. You can disable Cloud in Steam; the game continues to save data locally. Actual synchronization depends on Steam services and relevant settings.

LAN and Steam multiplayer

When hosting or joining a LAN game, the game communicates with other players on the same local network. LAN traffic is not encrypted. Room codes are intended only to prevent accidental entry; do not reuse an account password.

When you use Steam features, Valve's services handle related Steam identity, friends, lobbies, network connections and achievement data. Match traffic may connect directly or be relayed through Valve's network. Valve handles related data under its Steam Privacy Policy; these features depend on service availability and settings. A3 does not receive your Steam password or Steam Guard credentials.

Contacting support

You may voluntarily send email to 3173374170@qq.com. Your email address, message and any screenshots, logs or other attachments you choose to send are used to understand, address and reply to your request. Email service providers also process data involved in transmitting and storing email. Send only information needed to resolve the issue, and do not provide account passwords or Steam Guard codes.

Website visits

These website pages have no contact forms, analytics scripts, advertising trackers, third-party fonts or scripts, and do not set cookies. Email links open your mail application only when you select them; they do not send email automatically. This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitor IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not a visitor is signed into GitHub. See the GitHub Privacy Statement and GitHub Pages data collection information for details.

Third-party software

The Windows game build uses the Steamworks SDK, Qt and FFmpeg-compatible multimedia libraries to run the game, provide Steam connectivity and decode local audio. The developer does not use these components for advertising or behavioural tracking.

Privacy questions

For questions about this policy or information you voluntarily provided to support, contact 3173374170@qq.com.
